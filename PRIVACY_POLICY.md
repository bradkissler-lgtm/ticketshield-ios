# Privacy Policy — ParkShield

**Last updated:** 18 September 2026  
**Contact:** bkissler@vancap.com

ParkShield is a parking-sign reminder app for iPhone. It is built so that **sign photos and reminder times stay on your device**.

## What the app does

You photograph a parking or street-cleaning sign (or choose a photo). Apple’s Vision framework reads the text **on your iPhone** and suggests days and hours. You confirm the schedule. ParkShield then schedules a **local notification** before the restriction window you confirmed.

## Data we collect

ParkShield does **not** create an account and does **not** operate a ParkShield server for this app.

We do not collect, sell, or share:

- names, email addresses, or phone numbers (unless you email us)
- precise location
- parking-sign photos
- OCR text
- reminder schedules

Photos you take or pick are processed on device to suggest a schedule. They are not uploaded to VanCap, ParkShield, or any other cloud service by this app. After you confirm a schedule, ParkShield stores the label, days, hours, reminder lead time, and a short copy of the recognized text in **on-device** storage (SwiftData). That store is not synced to iCloud by this app.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — only to photograph a sign you point at. Optional; you can pick an existing photo or type the schedule.
- **Photos** — only to let you choose an existing sign photo. ParkShield does not add photos to your library.
- **Notifications** — local alerts before a restriction you confirmed, and (if you buy Pro and turn it on) an optional weekly digest. ParkShield does not send push notifications from a server.

You can deny any of these. The app still works for manual schedules; reminders will not appear if notifications are off.

## Purchases

Optional Pro upgrades are sold as Apple In-App Purchases (`ticketshield.pro.lifetime`, `ticketshield.pro.annual`). Payment is processed by Apple. ParkShield does not see your credit-card number. Restore Purchases uses your Apple ID via StoreKit.

Apple’s privacy policy applies to the App Store and your Apple ID: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

## Analytics, advertising, tracking

ParkShield does not include a third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking framework. It does not use App Tracking Transparency because it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Children

The app is age-rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal data.

## Email

If you write to **bkissler@vancap.com**, we receive whatever you put in that message so we can reply. We do not add you to a marketing list because you used the app.

## Data retention and deletion

On-device spots and settings are deleted when you delete the spot or delete the app. Uninstalling ParkShield removes local schedules and pending local notifications.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the copy distributed with the app.
