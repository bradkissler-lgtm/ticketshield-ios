The app

ParkShield suggests a parking or street-cleaning schedule from a sign you photograph or choose, then schedules a local reminder after you confirm the days and hours. It does not create an account, read a city database, or pay tickets. A reminder is not a promise that you will avoid a citation. Check the sign yourself. ParkShield is not affiliated with any city.

Free and Pro

Free includes one saved spot and reminder lead times of 15, 30, or 60 minutes. ParkShield Pro adds unlimited saved spots, a custom lead time from 5 to 120 minutes, and an optional weekly digest. Pro is offered two ways:

  • Pro Lifetime (ticketshield.pro.lifetime) — one-time In-App Purchase of $4.99. It does not auto-renew.
  • Pro Annual (ticketshield.pro.annual) — auto-renewable subscription, length 1 year, price $19.99 per year.

The price shown in the app at purchase is the price charged by Apple for your storefront. $19.99 and $4.99 are the United States prices.
