Contact

Email Bradley Kissler at bkissler@vancap.com. Include your iOS version and what you expected the reminder to do. There is no account to look up.

How reminders work

Photograph a parking or street-cleaning sign, or choose a photo. ParkShield suggests days and hours on your iPhone. You confirm the schedule. A local notification fires before the window (30 minutes by default; 15 or 60 on the free plan).

If notifications are off, open iOS Settings and allow them for ParkShield. The app does not send remote push notifications.

Moved the car

Open the spot and tap Moved car. That clears today’s alert for that spot. Next week’s reminder stays.

Free and Pro

  • Free: one saved spot, lead times of 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
  • Pro Lifetime: $4.99 once (ticketshield.pro.lifetime). Does not renew.
  • Pro Annual: $19.99 per year, auto-renewing, length 1 year (ticketshield.pro.annual).

Pro unlocks unlimited spots, a custom lead time from 5 to 120 minutes, and an optional weekly digest. Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings.

Cancel Pro Annual

Apple bills the subscription. Cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions at least 24 hours before the year ends. ParkShield cannot cancel an App Store subscription for you. Details are in the Terms of Use (EULA).
